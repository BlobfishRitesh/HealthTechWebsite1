Welcome to the HealthTech Club!

About Us

The HealthTech Club is a student-led organization focused on combing technology and engineering to solve real-world health and environmental problems.

Our members collaborate on innovative projects while learning new technical skills along the process. We also do various outreach activities to promote our club and engage with the community. This includes uploading skits to our tiktok and instagram pages in a cluely style format as well as informative videos about our projects and the club.

Innovation

Building projects that improve lives through technology

Impact
